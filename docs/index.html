R/V Vema, Voyage 24

For Dad, the computer technician aboard Vema on this voyage, who kept the ship's Digital PDP computer running as it turned passes of the new Transit navigation satellites into positions at sea.

In 1967 Lamont Geological Observatory's research schooner Vema sailed west-about around the world: New York, Panama, Hawaii, Midway, Manila, Australia, Mauritius, Cape Town, Rio and home to Miami. This page follows her day by day. It opens on today's date, 1967, and moves in real time.
